The Renard Fish Calendar

Eighteen months of the strangest fish ever painted, taken from Louis Renard’s 1754 Poissons, Ecrevisses et Crabes. Each month has a full-page plate, then a calendar page with moon phases, dates worth remembering and a short note. Tap any page to see it large; use the arrow keys to move through them.

I’d like your honest feedback. What works, what doesn’t, what you would change. Send me a note.
